How Hearth works

Everything you know about your community. One warm place.

Hearth is not a social media tool. It's not a CRM. It's a community manager's notebook — the place where everything worth knowing about your community actually lives.

✦ 01
The Hearth — your morning home base

Open Hearth in the morning. See what needs your attention today. Where does the community need you? What's happening? What can wait? All in one warm, unhurried view.

Daily pulse
✦ 02
The Chronicle — your people, their stories

A notebook for the members worth remembering. Not a database — a living record. The veteran who always shows up. The rising star worth watching. The context you need before a hard conversation.

Community CRM
✦ 03
The Gather — events without the chaos

Plan the whole lifecycle — hype, coordination, the event itself, the recap. Everything in one place. Nothing lost in Discord DMs or a Google Doc nobody can find.

✦ 04
The Voice — for when you do need to post

When content is part of the job, Hearth helps without replacing you. Kindling for your own ideas. Your voice, not a generic AI's. Nothing goes out without your eyes on it.

Optional · Human-first

Community managers pour their soul into spaces other people call home. We handle the mechanics so they never have to choose between consistency and authenticity.

The human is irreplaceable

Every feature enhances judgment — never bypasses it. You read the room. You know when not to post. No tool can replicate that and we will never try.

No guilt mechanics

Hearth never tells you what you haven't done. No streaks that break. No empty states that make you feel behind. Open it because you want to, not because it demands something.

Your space, not ours

Every workspace looks different because every community is. Customise the colours, the themes, the seasons. When someone sees your screenshot, they see you.

Built from the inside

Made by an ex-CM who lived every one of these problems. Not a growth hacker who read about community management once.

Our commitments

✕Anything that removes the human from the loop
✕Guilt mechanics that make you dread opening the app
✕Features built for Fortune 500 companies where individual voice is frowned upon
